Field Shift

A farmer-facing look at multi-year rotation options for Story County, Iowa, using NASA Earth observations, USDA CDL, and SoilGrids. This tool supports, and does not replace, local agronomic judgment.

Story County tiles

Each square is a 1.2 km analysis tile built from 30 m USDA CDL pixels. Click a tile, or pick a featured one. The map shows 2025 crop groups.

What each number means

Cover-crop “+cover” years assume a winter rye that shrinks bare-soil months and uses a little extra water. That assumption is documented, not observed as a CDL class. SMAP, ECOSTRESS, GPM IMERG and GRACE are named in the challenge and are not in this local demo because they need an Earthdata login.

Next-season crop, test years 2024–2025

Persistence is weak because Story County already alternates corn and soy. The practical bar is the 2nd-order Markov table. LightGBM is useful when it improves calibration and rare classes, not just headline accuracy.

Colab Full-County Run (2024–25)

A larger personal-Colab CPU run on the same Story County AOI and the same temporal split (train through 2021, validation 2022–2023, test 2024–2025). Seed 0, 25 Optuna trials, 400,000 sampled pixels (266,664 / 66,666 / 66,666), plus a 2025 full-county map over about 1.50 million farmed pixels. Shown here as evidence alongside the local Story County run above, not as a replacement for it. Both runs are labeled one seed, not a published benchmark.
